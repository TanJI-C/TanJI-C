## About me

I’m a Ph.D. student at the **University of Electronic Science and Technology of China**, advised by [Prof. Kai Zheng](https://zheng-kai.com/). My research spans **Agentic RL**, **AI4DB**, and **Physical AI**. I work on query optimization and autonomous database operations.

My earlier publications appear under the name **Junming Chen (陈俊明)**.
